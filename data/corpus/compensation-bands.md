# Salary Bands
Level 3 engineers have a base salary band of $78,000 to $98,000. Level 4 engineers have a band of $96,000 to $124,000. Level 5 senior engineers have a band of $118,000 to $152,000.

# Bonus and Equity
The target annual bonus is 10 percent of base salary for levels 3 and 4 and 15 percent for level 5. Equity refresh grants are reviewed once per year in March. Offers above the top of a band require approval from the Vice President of People.

# Pay Review
The merit increase budget for the annual review is 3.5 percent of total base payroll. Managers submit pay recommendations by 15 February. Individual salary information is confidential and must not be shared outside the approval chain.
