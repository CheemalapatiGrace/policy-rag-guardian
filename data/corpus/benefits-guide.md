# Health Coverage
The company pays 90 percent of the health insurance premium for employees and 75 percent for dependents. New hires must enroll within 30 days of their start date or wait for the next open enrollment period. Dental and vision plans are available at no additional cost to employees.

# Retirement
The company matches 401(k) contributions up to 4 percent of salary. Matching contributions vest immediately. Employees become eligible to contribute on the first day of the month after they join.

# Learning and Wellness
Every employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per year for courses, books and conferences. The wellness stipend is $40 per month and can be used for gym memberships or fitness apps. Unused learning budget does not carry over to the following year.
