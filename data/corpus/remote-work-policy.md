# Hybrid Schedule
Most roles follow a hybrid schedule with 3 days per week in the office. Teams choose their anchor days together with their manager, and everyone is expected to be reachable during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a vice president.

# Home Office Support
New remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500. The company also reimburses $50 per month toward internet costs. Equipment such as monitors and chairs remains company property and must be returned on departure.

# Working From Abroad
Employees may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year. Working abroad requires prior approval from HR and a tax review by Finance. Employees must not work from a sanctioned country under any circumstances.
