# Passwords and Authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across systems. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN and every production system. Employees must store passwords in the company password manager and never write them down or share them.

# Devices
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal USB storage devices are not permitted on company equipment. Report a lost or stolen device to the security desk within 1 hour of noticing the loss.

# Software and Networks
Only software on the approved software list may be installed on company devices. Employees must connect through the company VPN whenever they use public Wi-Fi. Employees must never bypass web filtering or security tooling.

# Phishing and Reporting
Report suspected phishing emails with the Report Phish button in the mail client. Employees who clicked a suspicious link must notify the security desk immediately and must not attempt their own clean-up. Annual security awareness training is mandatory and must be completed within 30 days of joining and every 12 months afterwards.
