# Retention Periods
Financial and tax records are retained for 7 years. Employee HR records are retained for 6 years after employment ends. Customer support tickets are retained for 2 years, and general business email is retained for 3 years.

# Security and System Data
Security and audit logs are retained for 1 year. Backups are retained for 90 days and then overwritten. Application debug logs must not contain personal data and are deleted after 30 days.

# Disposal
Data must be securely deleted within 30 days after its retention period expires. Paper records are destroyed by cross-cut shredding or a certified disposal vendor. Hardware is wiped to a recognised standard before reuse or recycling.

# Legal Holds
A legal hold issued by the Legal team overrides every retention period in this policy. Data under legal hold must not be deleted until Legal confirms the hold is released. Employees who receive a legal hold notice must preserve all related documents and messages.
