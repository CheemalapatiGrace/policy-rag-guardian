# Purchase Thresholds
Purchases above $10,000 require 3 competitive quotes. Purchases above $50,000 must be approved by the procurement committee, which meets on the first Tuesday of each month. Purchases below $10,000 may be approved by a department head with a single quote.

# Contracts and Vendor Risk
All vendor contracts must be reviewed by Legal before signature. Vendors that process company data must pass a security assessment before onboarding. Contracts with automatic renewal clauses must be reviewed 90 days before the renewal date.
