# Annual Leave
Full-time employees at Acme Dynamics receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month, and part-time employees accrue leave pro rata to their contracted hours. Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused days into the next calendar year, and carried-over days expire on 31 March.

# Requesting Leave
Submit every leave request through the HR portal at least 2 weeks before the first day of leave. Your manager must approve or decline the request within 3 business days. Requests longer than 10 consecutive days require approval from your department head as well.

# Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year that do not carry over. A doctor's note is required when sick leave lasts more than 3 consecutive days. Notify your manager before the start of your shift on the first day of any sick absence.

# Parental Leave
The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, and the secondary caregiver receives 8 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave must start within 12 months of the birth or adoption. Employees can return part-time for up to 4 weeks after parental leave ends.

# Bereavement and Public Holidays
Employees receive 5 paid days of bereavement leave for an immediate family member and 2 days for extended family. Acme Dynamics observes 11 public holidays each year, and the list is published on the HR portal every December.
