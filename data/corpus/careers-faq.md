# Hiring Process
Our hiring process has 3 interview rounds: a recruiter screen, a technical or role interview, and a final panel. Candidates hear back within 10 business days of each round. Acme Dynamics is an equal opportunity employer and welcomes applicants of all backgrounds.

# Internships and Locations
The internship program runs for 12 weeks each summer and applications open in January. Acme Dynamics has offices in Austin, Dublin and Singapore. Many roles are hybrid, with details listed on each job posting.
