# Respectful Workplace
Acme Dynamics has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination or bullying of any kind. Employees who experience or witness misconduct should report it to their manager, to HR or to the ethics hotline. Reports may be made anonymously through the web form at ethics@acmedynamics.example.

# Non-Retaliation and Investigations
Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited and is itself grounds for dismissal. The Ethics Office acknowledges every report within 3 business days. Investigations are normally completed within 30 days, and the outcome is shared with the reporter where possible.

# Gifts and Conflicts of Interest
Employees may not accept gifts worth more than $100 from vendors or customers. Any gift above that limit must be declined or reported to the Ethics Office. Employees must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest to their manager as soon as they arise.
