# Submitting Expenses
Submit expense reports within 30 days of the purchase date through the finance portal. Receipts are required for every expense above $25. Expenses submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed without CFO approval.

# Approval Thresholds
Managers approve expense reports up to $500. Any single expense above $500 requires approval from the department head before the purchase is made. Reimbursement is paid in the next payroll cycle after approval.

# Travel
Book flights in economy class for any trip shorter than 6 hours of flying time. The hotel cap is $220 per night in standard cities. The meal allowance while travelling is $60 per day, and alcohol is never reimbursed.

# Mileage and Transport
Use of a personal car for business is reimbursed at $0.55 per mile. Employees should use ride-share or public transport for airport transfers when the cost is lower than a rental car. Rental cars must be mid-size or smaller unless a manager approves otherwise.
